# USP-004: Remittance Routing AI

## Status

- **Type**: Unique Selling Point
- **Priority**: Could Have
- **Status**: Draft

## Description

Based on historical FX rates and volatility analysis in the `fx-service`, the system suggests the optimal time to send money home.

## Functional Alignment

- **FR-004**: Leverages historical FX data.
- **AI-003**: Part of the "FX-Aware Savings" strategy.

## Key Features

1. **Strategic Alerts**: "Rates for EUR -> COP are currently at a 3-month high; consider sending your monthly remittance today."
2. **Transfer Forecasting**: Predicts future rate movements using simple trend analysis to advise on "Wait vs. Send" decisions.
3. **Volatility Shield**: Suggests splitting large transfers into smaller batches during high-volatility periods.

## Business Value

Saves users money by optimizing the timing of their global financial flows, making the ledger a "profit-generating" tool rather than just a tracking tool.
