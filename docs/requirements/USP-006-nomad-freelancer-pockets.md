# USP-006: Nomad/Freelancer "Pocket" Segregation

## Status

- **Type**: Unique Selling Point
- **Priority**: Should Have
- **Status**: Draft

## Description

Enables digital nomads and freelancers to treat one tenant as a "Business" and another as "Personal," with automated workflows for inter-tenant transfers and reimbursements.

## Functional Alignment

- **FR-001**: Uses multi-tenancy for logical business/personal split.
- **FR-006**: Remittance linkage can be used to track "owner draws" or business expense reimbursements.

## Key Features

1. **Inter-Tenant Reimbursement**: Automated workflow to mark a personal expense as a "Business Reimbursement" and track the corresponding transfer from the business tenant.
2. **Pro-rata Expense Splitting**: Logic to split expenses (e.g., rent, internet) between personal and business tenants based on a fixed percentage.
3. **Switchable Identity**: Seamlessly switch between business and personal dashboard views without re-logging.

## Business Value

Targets the high-growth nomad/freelancer market by solving the specific pain point of blending personal and professional finances across multiple currencies.
