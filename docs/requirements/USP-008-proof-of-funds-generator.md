# USP-008: Proof of Funds Generator

## Status

- **Type**: Unique Selling Point
- **Priority**: Should Have
- **Status**: Draft

## Description

Provide a digitally signed "Proof of Wealth" or "Proof of Income" document that expats can use for visa applications, apartment rentals, or school enrollments.

## Functional Alignment

- **FR-011**: Leverages multi-currency balances to show aggregate wealth.
- **FR-015**: Extends export capabilities to include signed documents.

## Key Features

1. **Digital Signatures**: Reports are digitally signed by the platform to ensure authenticity.
2. **Aggregated Proof**: Combines balances from all linked bank accounts into a single "Net Worth" statement in a target currency (e.g., USD or EUR).
3. **Verification Portal**: A short-lived public URL (gated by a code on the PDF) that allows third parties (landlords, visa agents) to verify the document's validity.

## Business Value

Solves a major logistical hurdle for expats—proving financial status in a new country without having to provide dozens of separate bank statements.
